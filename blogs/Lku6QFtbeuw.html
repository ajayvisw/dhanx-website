The investment question

Why VWO can show a gap between underlying earnings growth and past returns.

An ETF is a wrapper around a collection of businesses and markets. Understanding its holdings, fees, diversification, underlying earnings and valuation helps explain what an investor owns beyond a headline return number.

How to use this idea

Past return can be useful context, but it does not establish future value. Cost, liquidity, structure and the investor’s broader allocation should be assessed before making a decision.
