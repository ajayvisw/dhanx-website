Long-term portfolios are built to survive uncertainty, not simply to express a view. Liquidity needs, concentration, valuation, time horizon and an investor’s ability to hold through a drawdown all matter alongside the attractiveness of an asset.

How to use this idea

A practical next step is to write down why the holding belongs in the portfolio, what could invalidate the thesis, and how large the position should be if that uncertainty remains.

The goal of this written brief is to make the central question easier to revisit and test over time. The accompanying video contains the complete discussion, examples and supporting context.
