How low-cost drones and AI are changing the economics of defence and military procurement.

Technology can alter the economics of an industry before the eventual winners are obvious. The first-order opportunity attracts attention, but costs, competition, regulation and capital intensity often determine who captures the value.

How to use this idea

For investors, that argues for separating the technology from the business model. A major change can be real while some individual investments still carry more risk than their price suggests.

The goal of this written brief is to make the central question easier to revisit and test over time. The accompanying video contains the complete discussion, examples and supporting context.
