The metric in this article is a tool for asking better questions, not a formula that can replace judgement. Good analysis combines financial measures with business quality, balance-sheet strength, cyclicality and the price paid.

How to use this idea

The most useful habit is to make the assumptions explicit. That makes it easier to test a conclusion when new results arrive and to avoid confusing a familiar ratio with a complete investment case.

The goal of this written brief is to make the central question easier to revisit and test over time. The accompanying video contains the complete discussion, examples and supporting context.
