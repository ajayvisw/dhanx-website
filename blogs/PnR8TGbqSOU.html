Five fault lines worth watching

This short explains why the presenter is holding more cash without making a doomsday call. The concern is not one headline; it is the way several fragile parts of the market can reinforce one another. AI capital expenditure is the first fault line: the spending is enormous, while the route from infrastructure spend to durable returns is still being tested.

Cash is a risk-management decision

The point is not that an investor can time a crash. It is that a portfolio should leave room for uncertainty when valuations, leverage and the pace of investment all look demanding. Holding cash can be a conscious choice to preserve flexibility, rather than a prediction that every asset must fall.
