The investment question

A research note on market pressure facing software companies during a shift in AI expectations and valuation assumptions.

Markets tend to reward a compelling narrative long before the economic outcome is certain. The useful work is to identify the inputs that would have to be true for today’s valuation to make sense: capital spending, pricing power, debt capacity, margins and eventual free cash flow.

How to use this idea

Rather than treating a theme as an all-or-nothing call, investors can ask which companies bear the most execution risk, which have balance-sheet resilience, and whether their position size still reflects uncertainty. That process does not predict a turning point; it creates a clearer decision framework.

The goal of this written brief is to make the central question easier to revisit and test over time. The accompanying video contains the complete discussion, examples and supporting context.
