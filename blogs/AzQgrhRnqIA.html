A company is more than its recent share-price move. A useful research process starts with the business model, sources of revenue, competitive advantage, reinvestment opportunities, financial strength and the risks that could change the story.

How to use this idea

Valuation then becomes a question of expectations. Investors can compare the growth and profitability implied by the current price with what the company has historically delivered and what a realistic competitive environment might permit.

The goal of this written brief is to make the central question easier to revisit and test over time. The accompanying video contains the complete discussion, examples and supporting context.
