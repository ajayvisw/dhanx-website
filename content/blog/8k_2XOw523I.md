# NSE's next growth engine: what Nasdaq became

NSE is listing at roughly **43× FY26 earnings**. The question that multiple raises
is not whether the exchange is dominant — it is — but where the growth after
options comes from.

Two global exchanges show the available paths, and they diverged.

## CME stayed an exchange

CME Group remains transaction-led: approximately **$5.3 billion of its ~$6.5
billion 2025 revenue** came from clearing and transaction fees.

That is a perfectly good business, earning excellent returns. It is simply the same
business, larger.

## Nasdaq became something else

Nasdaq is now a data and financial technology company that also owns exchanges.
Only about **23% of net revenue comes from Market Services**.

The rest:

- **Capital Access** — indices, listings, data: **41%**
- **Financial Technology** — **35%**

Most of Nasdaq's revenue no longer depends on how much anybody trades.

## Where NSE sits today

Non-trading revenue, from Q4 FY26 disclosures:

- Data connectivity — **₹1,129 Cr**
- Data feeds — **₹470 Cr**
- Listings — **₹352 Cr**
- Co-location racks — **₹205 Cr**
- Index licensing — **₹152 Cr**

Real, but small beside roughly ₹13,000 crore of total revenue.

## Why this revenue is better than it looks

It is recurring, and it is sticky in a specific way.

When a quantitative firm wants Indian market data, it buys from NSE — the source,
not a reseller. Co-location is bought where the matching engine sits. Index
licensing follows whoever owns the benchmark.

None of that is contestable by a competitor offering a lower price, and contracts
renew at higher volumes as the firms grow.

## The actual bet

**The long-term case for NSE is not that Indians will trade weekly options
forever.** That revenue is concentrated, regulated, and already showing the strain
of intervention.

It is whether NSE can build Nasdaq-style recurring businesses — indices, data,
technology, infrastructure — on top of a liquidity network nobody can replicate.

If it does, 43× is a price for a larger addressable market. If it stays a
transaction business, 43× is a price for the same market with the growth already
in it.
