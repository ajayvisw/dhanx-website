Free, no sign-up

Seven calculators.
No account, no catch.

Open any of these and start typing. They run entirely in your browser, so the numbers you enter are never sent to us — and you do not need an account to get an answer. Sign up only if you want to keep a calculation and use it on another device.

Planning your independence
🎯

Financial Independence Calculator

The portfolio value at which work becomes optional, and the year you reach it, from your savings rate, expected return, inflation and withdrawal rate. It models the drawdown year by year rather than applying a flat multiple of your expenses, so a long retirement and a short one give different answers.

📈

Net Worth Projection

Where your net worth lands years from now, given what you hold today, what you add each month and what you expect to earn. Needs a free account, because it reads from your own tracked assets rather than numbers you re-type.

What your money actually returned
📊

XIRR Calculator

The money-weighted return across cash flows that happened on different dates — which is every SIP, every top-up and every partial redemption. A fund's published return assumes one lump sum held throughout; yours almost never was. Upload a CSV or Excel file, or type the flows by hand.

🏠

Property XIRR

What a property genuinely earned, once the down payment, every EMI, maintenance, property taxes, rent received and the eventual sale price are treated as dated cash flows. It is usually a smaller number than the headline price appreciation, and the gap is the point.

Property decisions
🔑

Rent vs Buy

Counts what the argument usually skips: stamp duty, registration, maintenance, and what your down payment would have earned if it had stayed invested instead.

⚖️

Real Estate vs Equities

The same money in property or in an index, compared across four real strategies, with loan interest, rental yield, vacancy and transaction costs on the property side.

🏘️

Real Estate Planning Hub

All three property tools in one place, if you would rather start from the question than from the calculator.

Day to day
💰

Budget Planner

A budget that starts from what you want the money to buy, rather than from a list of categories to cut. Built around the idea that a plan you will actually follow beats a stricter one you will not.

🏁

Growth of $1

Race the long-run total return of stocks, funds and indices against each other, dividends included, on real monthly history. Needs a free account, because it pulls live market data.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Not for the seven free calculators. Open one and use it. An account is only needed to keep a saved calculation and reach it from another device — and the two tools above that read live market data or your own holdings.

Where do my numbers go?

Nowhere. The free calculators compute entirely in your browser and make no call to a server, so what you type never reaches us. Anything you save lives in that browser alone, which also means clearing your browsing data clears it.

Want your calculations to follow you?

A free account keeps saved scenarios across devices, and connects your Indian and US holdings into one net-worth view so the projections start from real numbers instead of estimates.
