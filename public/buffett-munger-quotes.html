Buy wonderful businesses

How these quotes were chosen

Investing quotes are badly polluted online, and Buffett and Munger are misquoted more than almost anyone. So a line appears here only if it can be traced, word for word, to a primary source:

  • Warren Buffett — the Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters, 1977 onward.
  • Charlie Munger — his talks and interviews as published in Poor Charlie’s Almanack, and lines Buffett quotes from him in the letters.

The “In plain words” lines are Dhan-X’s own short explanations, not part of the quotes. Square brackets mark a word added for sense.

Some widely shared lines are absent — “Rule No. 1: never lose money”, “the stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient”, “the big money is not in the buying and selling, but in the waiting” — not because they are necessarily false, but because we could not find them in these sources. If you know where one was said or written, we would like to add it.
