Motivation from two great investors

Warren Buffett & Charlie Munger,
in their own words

75 quotes on investing, patience and how to live, from more than four decades of Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters and Charlie Munger’s talks. Every line is checked word for word against the letter or talk it comes from, and links to the original.

How these quotes were chosen

Investing quotes are badly polluted online, and Buffett and Munger are misquoted more than almost anyone. So this page only includes a line if it can be traced, word for word, to a primary source:

  • Warren Buffett — the Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters, 1977 onward, linked to the year each quote appears.
  • Charlie Munger — his talks and interviews as published in Poor Charlie’s Almanack, and lines Buffett quotes from him in the letters.

Square brackets mark a word added for sense. Several widely shared lines are absent — “Rule No. 1: never lose money”, “the stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient”, “the big money is not in the buying and selling, but in the waiting” — not because they are necessarily false, but because we could not find them in these sources. If you know where one was said or written, we would like to add it.
